Your screen's sub-pixel order

The LCD samples were rendered for horizontal RGB stripes, the usual order of desktop LCDs. On a screen with another order the colour fringes land on the wrong sub-pixels: the text looks rough and coloured instead of sharper. Below is the same text rendered for RGB (left) and BGR (right), shown at exactly one device pixel per sample pixel — small on a high-density screen, so look closely. Click the one that reads as clean black text.
