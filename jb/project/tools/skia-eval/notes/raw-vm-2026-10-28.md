**Build (§1): two Windows-only breaks on the first build of the raster surface, fixed on `…-windows10`**

- `SKRaster.obj`: `BufImgSurfaceData.h` includes `colordata.h`, which on Windows lives in
  `windows/native/libawt/windows/` (the unix copy is in `common/awt`, which the Mac's header list has).
  `libawt/windows` added to the Skia library's header dirs for Windows (`AwtLibraries.gmk`).
- `SKRaster.cpp` static-asserts an RGBA Skia build; the Windows Skia build is BGRA (`SkTypes.h`:
  `SK_R32_SHIFT 16` under `SK_BUILD_FOR_WIN`, so `kN32 == kBGRA_8888`). Red step (assertion removed, nothing
  else): 6 of 8 `SkiaRaster*Test` files fail — red and blue exchanged in every fill (Surface "AA oval, opaque:
  max 164 levels", Paint gradients max 224, Stroke max 170, NonAA 3743 interior pixels, Mirror, Text's grayscale
  rows). Fix: `kSwizzle = (kN32 == kRGBA_8888)`; the swizzle and the BGR geometry declaration apply on RGBA
  builds only, a BGRA build takes Java's ARGB ints as Skia's pixels and declares `kRGB_H`. Green: 7 of 8.
  Commit `58eec3997377`.
- `java -Dsun.java2d.skia.raster=true -Djava.awt.headless=true` prints nothing to stderr (the census and the
  benches run under it).

**Open: `ClipShapeTest` with dashes under the raster flag.** The test renders every random path twice — Marlin's
runtime clipping on and off (`sun.java2d.renderer.clip.runtime`) — and expects the two images equal within 32
levels on at most 4 pixels. Under the flag a dashed stroke is Marlin's clipped outline (`RenderingEngine.strokeTo`
with the clip, as `LoopPipe.getStrokeSpans`) filled by Skia (`SKRasterShapePipe`, the dash branch). Its 116 saved
pairs (`pngdiff.py` over the jtreg scratch): 7276 differing pixels, 294 beyond 32, max 191, in 77 pairs; the
differences sit on dash edges across the whole 100 × 100 image (boxes span it), 15–280 pixels per pair, i.e. the two
images are drawn with different edge coverage, not with shifted dashes. **Not reproduced by `DashClipProbe`**
(the same stroke — width 10, dashes 13/7, all caps and joins, AA, STROKE_PURE — on nine-line polylines in the test's
coordinate range, 400 paths): clip on vs off **identical to the pixel** on the raster surface, while raster vs the
loops differs by up to 216 levels on 200 of 400 paths — edge coverage, the images look alike (`dashclip-wide/`).
What the test does that the probe does not: `clearRect` with AA off between paths on a long-lived Graphics2D,
`KEY_RENDERING = QUALITY`, 5000 paths per setup. No red `SkiaRaster*Test` yet, therefore no fix; next step is to
run the test's own loop with the flag and dump one failing pair's outline paths. The dashed case may also simply be
worth routing differently (the test's whole premise is that both renderings are Marlin's).

**Finding, fixed: text through Skia's text route on INT_ARGB was 86× the INT_ARGB_PRE time.** The bench's text
rows with `-Dsun.java2d.font.scaler=skia -Dsun.java2d.skia.text=skia`: INT_ARGB_PRE 4.99 / 5.82 ms (gray / LCD),
**INT_ARGB 369.8 / 332.9 ms**. Cause: `SKRasterTextPipe.draw` handed the natives the *clip* as the operation's box;
on INT_ARGB the premultiplied mirror is written back for the box after every operation — the whole 1200 × 900
image per string. Fix on `…-windows10` (`f5846f822d83`): the box is the glyph list's image bounds

`SkiaRasterTextCostTest` (400 strings, INT_ARGB vs INT_ARGB_PRE) ratio 86 (limit 3); green 1.9 (7.5 vs 4.0 ms).
The remaining factor is the mirror's import and write-back of the text's own pixels. The nine other files pass.
