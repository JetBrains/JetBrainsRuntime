- Reading, against the VM (ARM64) and the Mac:
  - AA fills are faster here as there: on INT_RGB and INT_ARGB_PRE ovals 0.44 / 0.51, round rects 0.58 / 0.68,
    cubic blobs 0.60 / 0.72; bicubic images 0.66-0.71 on every type (the VM's direction, not the Mac's).
  - Slower, and more so than on the VM: non-AA ovals, round rects and strokes 1.2-1.9x on INT_RGB /
    INT_ARGB_PRE (the VM: 0.98-1.44 on INT_ARGB_PRE), integer rectangles 1.43x (VM 0.90), small AA
    rectangles through the converter 1.6x (VM 0.76). The loops are relatively fast on this core (0.43 ms for
    2000 integer rectangles, about 0.2 µs each); the raster surface adds about 90 ns per rectangle on top.
  - INT_ARGB (the mirror path) is slower almost throughout: AA shapes 0.68-0.98, non-AA 1.6-2.9x, small AA
    rectangles 3.5x; consistent with the fixed-cost test's marginal 1.5 limit above.
  - Text: with the loops' glyph images the raster surface is at parity (0.93-1.06); Skia's text route costs
    1.1x (LCD on INT_RGB) to 2.1x (gray on INT_RGB), 1.5-1.6x on INT_ARGB_PRE and about 4x on INT_ARGB, where
    the VM saw 1.8-1.9x on INT_ARGB_PRE and 2.7x on INT_ARGB.
  - For the IDE (INT_RGB back buffer): what it draws most (text with the loops' glyphs, rectangles, icons) is
    at parity or slower, what it draws less (rounded AA borders, AA icons) faster; the scenes below weigh it.
